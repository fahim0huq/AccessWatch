# AccessWatch outputs - field dictionary

Event: `mozambique_flood_2026` | NISAR before 2026-01-05 / after 2026-01-17 | term `HHHH`
Rasters: GeoTIFF in EPSG:32736 at 20 m. Vectors: GeoJSON in EPSG:4326. Web overlays: PNG in EPSG:4326 (bounds in `web/overlay_bounds.json`).

## Rasters (`flood/`)
| file | values |
|---|---|
| `flood_mask.tif` | 1 = new flood (permanent water excluded), 0 = none |
| `flood_veg_candidates.tif` | 1 = flooded-vegetation / urban candidate; part of `flood_mask.tif` only if `include_flooded_vegetation` was on |
| `depth.tif` | estimated depth in metres inside the flood, NaN elsewhere (an ESTIMATE) |
| `depth_class.tif` | 0 dry, 1 shallow (<0.3 m), 2 medium (<0.8 m), 3 deep, 4 unknown |
| `depth_confidence.tif` | 0 not flooded, 1 low, 2 medium, 3 high |

Also in `flood/`: `flood_baseline_threshold.tif` (1 = flood from the threshold map, before the classifier), `flood_raw.tif`, `flood_confidence.tif` (0 not flooded, 1 low, 2 medium, 3 high), `flood_probability.tif` (0-1), and `flood_polygons.geojson` (Polygon, `area_ha`; some rings self-intersect, so repair before use).

## `pockets/pockets.geojson` (Polygon per cut-off pocket)
`pocket_id` P01.. | `rank` 1 = go first | `priority_score` = pop_norm x service_loss_norm x difficulty_norm (each 0-1; the app may re-weight them) |
`population` ESTIMATED people | `status` `cut_off` (needs boat) or `difficult` (truck / on foot) |
`access_mode` `normal_vehicle` / `high_clearance_truck` / `on_foot` / `boat` | `boat_leg_km` length of the water leg |
`clinic_access`, `market_access`, `shelter_access`, `aid_depot_access`: `yes` / `difficult` / `no` / `n/a` |
`lost_services`, `limited_services` comma separated (`aid_depot_link` = the link to the depot) |
`risk_level` low / medium / high (worst condition on the safest route) | `max_depth_class` on the safest route |
`confidence` high / medium / low (weakest blocking road) | `stability` and `mode_stability` 0-1 = share of sensitivity scenarios in which the pocket / its access mode survives | `worst_case_mode` = most demanding access mode in any scenario (prepare it as a fallback) |
`route_shortest_*`, `route_safest_*` distance (km) and time (min) | `lon`, `lat` representative point |
`nearest_place`, `place_direction`, `place_distance_m` where the pocket is | `pop_norm`, `service_loss_norm`, `difficulty_norm` the priority terms.

## `routes/routes.geojson` (LineString, two per pocket)
`pocket_id`, `rank`, `route_type` `shortest` (dry network, ignores the flood) or `safest` (lowest risk cost inside the chosen access mode) |
`access_mode` | `distance_km`, `estimated_time_min` | `max_depth_class`, `km_shallow`, `km_medium`, `km_deep` | `boat_leg_km` | `uncertain_bridges` | `risk_score` (km-equivalents), `risk_level`.

## `network/road_conditions.geojson` (LineString, one per road piece of at most 100 m)
`edge_id`, `orig_eid`, `road_type`, `name`, `length_m` | `depth_class` 0-3 (+ `depth_class_name`), `max_depth_m`, `flooded`, `flooded_frac`, `depth_confidence` |
`is_bridge`, `bridge_uncertain` (bridge or ford near flood water: verify on the ground) | `access_normal_vehicle`, `access_high_clearance`, `access_foot` | `travel_time_min`, `risk_cost`.

## `mission_cards.json`
`cards[]` sorted by rank: `rank`, `pocket_id`, `title`, `location{lat, lon, nearest_place, direction_from_place}`, `population`, `population_note`, `access_status`, `access_mode`, `boat_leg_km`, `lost_services[]{service, previous_minutes}`, `limited_services[]`, `service_access{clinic, market, shelter, aid_depot}`, `routes{safest, shortest}` (each `distance_km`, `time_min`, `max_depth_class`, `risk_level`, `risk_score`, `uncertain_bridges`), `max_depth_class`, `risk_level`, `confidence`, `verification_required`, `verify_notes[]`, `short_water_crossing`, `depot_auto_selected`, `priority_score`, `priority_terms{pop_norm, service_loss_norm, difficulty_norm}`, `summary`, `alert_text` (short text for field teams), `data`, `stability`, `worst_case_mode`.

## `network/services.geojson` and `network/aid_depot.geojson` (Point)
Services: `type` `clinic` / `market` / `shelter`, `name` (may be empty; OSM may list one facility twice), `on_network`. Depot: `name`.

## App bundle: `accesswatch_mobile/assets/bundle/`
Generated by `python tools/build_bundle.py` (dependencies: `tools/requirements.txt`; never edit by hand). All GeoJSON is EPSG:4326, minified UTF-8, 5-decimal coordinates. The build stops, leaving the old bundle in place, if any check fails.

**`manifest.json`**: `schema_version`, `event`, `synthetic_demo` | `bounds` (raster box) and `bounds_all` (also roads, pockets, routes, services), each `west`, `south`, `east`, `north` | `dates`: `nisar_before`, `nisar_after`, `ems_image`, `ems_hours_after_nisar`, `notebook_generated` | `quality_flags` (copy of `quality.flags`) | `depth_classes`: `source` (where the limits came from), `shallow` (`max_m`), `medium` (`min_m`, `max_m`), `deep` (`min_m`), checked against `depth.tif` at build time | `compare`: `pixels` (`both`, `only_ours`, `only_ems`, counted on the 20 m grid inside the EMS mapped area), `pixel_area_m2`, `iou`, `our_flood_pixels_outside_ems_area`, `image` (`width`, `height`), `colors` | `graph`: `nodes`, `edges`, `self_loops_dropped`, `routes_replayed`, `connectivity` (`all`, `normal_vehicle`, `high_clearance`, `foot`, `dry`, each `components`, `largest`) | `stats`: `flood_area_km2`, `pockets`, `population_estimate`, `pockets_by_access_mode`, `flooded_road_pieces` (`shallow`, `medium`, `deep`), `clinic_status` (count per status) | `files`: per file `sha256`, `size` (every file except the manifest).

**`quality.json`**: `flags` booleans that drive the app's translated warnings: `nisar_beta`, `depot_auto_selected`, `validation_chosen_by_product_order`, `classifier_calibrated_on_ems`, `depot_assumed` (recognised for future runs; false in the current data) | `source_flags` original English text (traceability only, never shown) | `ems`: `product`, `layer`, `image_sensor`, `image_time`, `hours_after_nisar_after`, `aoi_share_of_bounds` | `notes`: `road_pieces_outside_raster`, `road_pieces_outside_raster_labelled_dry`, `pocket_share_outside_raster`, `pockets_where_safest_differs_in_routes`, `rank_population_inversions` (pairs: first ranks above second despite equal terms and fewer people), `clinic_records`, `clinic_record_pairs_within_100m` | `report` = `validation_report.json` and `sources` = `DATA_SOURCES.json` without `quality_flags`, both verbatim. Use `sources.validation_reference`, not the older `sources["Validation reference"]`, and `ems.image_time` for the date (`validation_reference.date` is null in the source).

**`services.geojson`** (Point): `service_id` (`S01`.. in source order, `D01` depot), `type` (`clinic`, `market`, `shelter`, `aid_depot`), `name`, `on_network` (not on the depot) | clinics only: `clinic_status`, `pocket_id` (null unless `inside_pocket`). First match wins: `inside_pocket` the clinic is inside a pocket polygon; `access_flooded` its nearest road node is off the largest dry road network; `reachable` otherwise. Measured from the dry network, not the depot. It does not say whether any pocket can reach the clinic.

**`graph.json`**: `lon[]`, `lat[]` give each node by index | `edges[]` is flat, repeating `edge_fields`: `u`, `v`, `length_m`, `depth_class` 0-3, `flags`, `risk_cost`, `travel_time_min` | `flag_bits`: `normal_vehicle` 1, `high_clearance` 2, `foot` 4, `bridge` 8, `bridge_uncertain` 16. Road pieces have no node ids, so nodes are their end points on the 5-decimal grid. `travel_time_min` is rounded to 0.1 at source, so summed times can be about a minute off: show `routes.geojson` times for published routes.

## Limits
- NISAR gives flood extent only; depth is an estimate (FwDET-style from a 30 m surface model) - classes are indicative.
- L-band thresholds detect open water well; flooded vegetation and built-up areas may be missed.
- OSM completeness: 99.0% of buildings are within 150 m of a mapped road; 2 disconnected road fragments were ignored.
- Population is WorldPop-style population raster - a modelled estimate, not a census.
- The boat route follows flooded roads; real water routes can differ. Bridges cannot be assessed from space.
- All depth / vehicle thresholds are adjustable defaults - confirm them with local responders and always verify on the ground.
